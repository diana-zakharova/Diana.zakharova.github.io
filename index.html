About Me

I am a PhD student in Biology with a background in Cell, Molecular Biology, and Genetics. My research interests center on cancer biology, cellular plasticity, and the molecular mechanisms that influence tumor progression and therapeutic response.


My research combines experimental and genomic approaches to investigate how cancer cells change their identity and develop vulnerabilities that may be therapeutically targeted.

Research Interests

Genomics & Epigenomics
