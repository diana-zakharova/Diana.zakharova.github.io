Research Interests

Exploring Therapeutic Vulnerabilities in Prostate Cancer

My research interests center on understanding the molecular biology of prostate cancer and using this knowledge to identify new opportunities for therapeutic intervention.

01

The Problem

Prostate cancer remains a significant challenge, and the effectiveness of existing treatments can be limited by the complex biology of cancer cells. Understanding the molecular processes that contribute to cancer progression can help reveal new opportunities for therapeutic intervention.

02

The Approach

My research interests involve combining functional experiments with molecular and genomic approaches to better understand cancer cell biology and identify potential therapeutic vulnerabilities.

03

The Goal

The broader goal of my research is to contribute to the development of new therapeutic strategies that can improve treatment options and ultimately benefit patients with prostate cancer.

Key Experimental Approaches

Cell-Based Assays

Assessing drug responses and changes in cancer cell behavior.

Flow Cytometry

Characterizing cell populations and molecular or genetic changes.

Genomic Profiling

Using approaches such as ATAC-seq and RNA-seq to study molecular changes.

In Vivo Models

Studying tumor biology and therapeutic responses in vivo.
