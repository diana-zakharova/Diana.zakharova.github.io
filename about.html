About Me

About Me

A little about my scientific background, the research environment I work in, and the things I enjoy outside the lab.

Your photo here

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Biology at Temple University. My research is carried out in the Feng Lab at Fox Chase Cancer Center, where I study cancer biology with a focus on prostate cancer.

I am interested in how cancer cells grow, adapt, and change their state, and in using molecular, cellular, genomic, and in vivo approaches to uncover vulnerabilities that could be therapeutically relevant.

My goal is to build a career in cancer research and contribute to a deeper understanding of tumor biology and the development of new therapeutic strategies.

The Feng Lab at Fox Chase Cancer Center

Our research uses experimental and genomic approaches to investigate mechanisms of cancer biology and therapeutic response. My work combines hands-on experimental research with approaches such as CRISPR, flow cytometry, genomic profiling, and in vivo models.
