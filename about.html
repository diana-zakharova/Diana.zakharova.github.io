About Me

About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Biology at Temple University, where I study cancer biology in the Feng Lab at Fox Chase Cancer Center. I previously graduated from Boston University with a B.A. in Cell, Molecular Biology, and Genetics, where I developed a broad foundation in the biological sciences and gained my first experiences in research.

My academic and research experiences have led me to pursue cancer biology, with a particular interest in the biological processes that determine how cancer cells grow, adapt, and respond to treatment. I am interested in combining experimental and genomic approaches to better understand these processes and identify vulnerabilities that could have therapeutic relevance.

My current research focuses on prostate cancer, where I use molecular, cellular, and in vivo approaches to investigate cancer biology and explore potential therapeutic opportunities.

My long-term goal is to become an independent researcher focused on developing new approaches to cancer treatment and translating discoveries in cancer biology into meaningful therapeutic advances.
