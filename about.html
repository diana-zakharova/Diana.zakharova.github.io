About Me

A background in molecular biology, genetics, and cancer research, with a focus on understanding cancer biology and therapeutic opportunities.

Your photo here

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Biology at Temple University. I previously graduated from Boston University with a B.A. in Cell, Molecular Biology, and Genetics, where I developed a broad foundation in the biological sciences and gained my first experiences in research.

My academic and research experiences led me to pursue cancer biology, particularly the biological processes that shape how cancer cells grow, adapt, and respond to treatment. I am interested in using experimental and genomic approaches to investigate these processes and uncover vulnerabilities that may be therapeutically relevant.

My current research focuses on prostate cancer, where I use molecular, cellular, and in vivo approaches to investigate cancer biology and potential therapeutic opportunities.

My long-term goal is to become an independent researcher focused on developing new approaches to cancer treatment and translating discoveries in cancer biology into meaningful therapeutic advances.
